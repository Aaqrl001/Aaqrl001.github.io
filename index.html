About the lab

The Aerosol and Air Quality Research Laboratory (AAQRL) is housed in the Department of Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering at Vanderbilt University. Our focus is on research and education related to particulates in gaseous suspension, known as aerosols. AAQRL’s contributions to this field span the full process of technology development, from synthesis and characterization to eventual application. Through this research, we aim to mitigate the negative impacts of aerosols on the environment and leverage their potential for positive contributions to society.

Research themes

Following threads run through our work.

Atmospheric Aerosol Chemistry

How aerosols nucleate, grow, and age in the atmosphere, and what that means for climate and health impacts.

Air Quality Sensing & Monitoring

Low-cost sensor networks and calibration methods that make high-resolution air quality data accessible.

Indoor & Urban Air Quality

Exposure and ventilation studies in homes, classrooms, and city environments.
