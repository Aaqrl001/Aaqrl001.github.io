Aerosol & Air Quality Research Laboratory

Understanding the particles that shape the air we breathe.

We study how aerosols form, travel, and affect health and climate — combining atmospheric chemistry, field measurement, and low-cost sensing to bring better air quality data to the places that need it most.

Research at a glance

Three threads run through our work: how particles form and evolve, how we can measure them better, and what they mean for the people breathing them.

View all research →

Atmospheric Aerosol Chemistry

How aerosols nucleate, grow, and age in the atmosphere, and what that means for their climate and health impacts.

Air Quality Sensing & Monitoring

Low-cost sensor networks and calibration methods that make high-resolution air quality data accessible beyond regulatory monitors.

Indoor & Urban Air Quality

Exposure and ventilation studies in homes, classrooms, and city environments where people spend most of their time.
