Upcoming and recent laboratory events, seminars, talks, and presentations.

Aerosol Science and Technology Enabling Synthesis. May 8, 2025. King Abdullah University of Science & Technology. KAUSTPSE Masterclass: Prof. Pratim Biswas.

Dr. Biswas discussed the use of sustainable precursors (byproducts from other processes) as feedstock in aerosol synthesis processes to produce functional nanomaterials. Such approaches help advance circular economies which lead to increased sustainability.
