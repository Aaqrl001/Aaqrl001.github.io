Research

Three threads, one atmosphere

From how a single particle nucleates to how a city's air quality index moves through the day, our research spans scales — connecting lab chemistry, field sensing, and public health.

01 — Aerosol Chemistry

Atmospheric Aerosol Chemistry

We investigate how aerosols nucleate, grow, and chemically age in the atmosphere — and how that evolution changes their optical properties, health impacts, and role in cloud formation. Current work combines chamber experiments with field campaigns to link molecular-level chemistry to atmospheric-scale outcomes.

02 — Sensing

Air Quality Sensing & Monitoring

Regulatory monitors are sparse and expensive. We develop and calibrate low-cost sensor networks that fill the gaps — giving communities and researchers access to high-resolution, real-time air quality data.

03 — Exposure

Indoor & Urban Air Quality

Most exposure happens indoors — homes, classrooms, vehicles. We study ventilation, infiltration, and indoor sources to understand real-world exposure and to inform interventions that actually reduce it.
