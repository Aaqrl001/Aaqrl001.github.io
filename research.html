Research Approach

AAQRL research connects fundamental aerosol science with instrumentation, technology development, and real-world applications.

Fundamental Science

Our research examines aerosol properties, particle behavior, chemistry, transport, and the physical mechanisms that govern aerosol processes.

Instrumentation & Measurement

We develop and apply aerosol measurement technologies, sensors, instruments, and analytical methods to characterize particles and their behavior.

Applications & Impact

Laboratory research is translated into technologies and applications addressing environmental, health, manufacturing, and engineering challenges.
